Built for Edge Resiliency

Engineered to function with zero external internet access, ensuring clinical continuity in remote PHCs.

⚡
100% Offline Local Inference
Runs local quantized models (`gemma2:2b` and `nomic-embed-text`) via Ollama. Operates entirely without cellular data, cloud APIs, or telemetry.
🎙️
On-Device Voice Triage
CHEWs and patients can speak directly using the microphone. Audio is transcribed offline with `faster-whisper` on standard CPU hardware with zero cloud transmission.
📚
Grounded Vector RAG
Every triage decision cites verified clinical protocols indexed into local ChromaDB storage, including suicide de-escalation, pesticide poisoning, and fever rule-outs.
🚨
Three-Tiered Triage Protocol
Instantly stratifies patients into Code Red (Emergency Stabilization & Transfer), Code Yellow (Urgent review within 2 hours), and Code Green (Routine care & counseling).
🔒
Sovereign Audit Logging
All clinical triage events are recorded to local append-only JSONL files (`audit_log.jsonl`) on disk for clinical review, with zero telemetry sent outside the facility.
🩺
Mandatory Medical Rule-Outs
Explicitly checks for underlying organic infections (cerebral malaria mRDT, typhoid, hypoglycemia, postpartum psychosis) before psychiatric attribution.

Human-Scale AI Principles

Why small, specialized models outperform generic cloud LLMs in rural healthcare delivery.

1. Small

Runs smoothly on standard consumer hardware (<15W power draw, 8GB RAM). Fits easily on a solar-powered mini PC or clinic laptop without costly cloud GPU servers.

2. Intentional

Refuses generic small-talk to focus exclusively on verified clinical workflows: rapid triage, lethal means restriction, and organic medical rule-outs.

3. Tailored

Engineered for the cultural and linguistic nuances of Nigerian PHCs, connecting grassroots health workers directly with WHO mhGAP guidelines.
