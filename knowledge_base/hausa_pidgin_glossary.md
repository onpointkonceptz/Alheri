# Multilingual Clinical Glossary: Hausa & Nigerian Pidgin Idioms of Distress

## 1. Cultural Context & Somatic Idioms
In Nigerian primary healthcare settings, psychological distress is frequently communicated through culturally patterned somatic complaints, metaphors, and cultural idioms rather than direct emotional vocabulary (like "depression" or "bipolar"). 

Clinicians and Community Health Extension Workers (CHEWs) must decode these expressions to avoid misdiagnosing psychiatric distress as non-specific infections or purely spiritual problems.

---

## 2. Hausa Language (Harshen Hausa) Clinical Idioms

| Hausa Expression | Literal Meaning | Clinical / Psychiatric Interpretation | Recommended Clinical Response |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Damuwa / Ina da damuwa** | Anxiety / Worry / I have troubles | Pervasive anxiety, situational stress, depressive distress. | Explore stressors: financial strain, marital friction, harvest loss, illness in the family. |
| **Bacin Ciki** | Bad stomach / spoilt belly | Deep sadness, grief, depressive episode, anger or heartbreak. | Inquire about mood changes, crying spells, appetite decrease, sleep loss. |
| **Zuciyata na tafasa / Bugun zuciya** | My heart is boiling / Rapid heart palpitation | Panic reaction, somatic anxiety, severe emotional turmoil, or PTSD arousal. | Check pulse and BP; conduct cardiac and thyroid checks; teach calm breathing. |
| **Kaina yana sara min / Zafin kai** | Splitting headache / Hot brain | Tension headache, somatization of stress, hyper-arousal. | Check blood pressure; assess sleep deprivation and dehydration; explore worries. |
| **Kasala / Rashin kuzari** | Total lethargy / Lack of strength | Anhedonia, psychomotor slowing, chronic depressive fatigue, severe anemia. | Evaluate hemoglobin/PCV; evaluate for vegetative depressive signs. |
| **Ina jin zafi a cikin jikina** | I feel internal heat inside my body | Common somatic depression/anxiety syndrome ("internal heat" sensation). | Rule out malaria, typhoid, menopause; validate symptoms as genuine distress. |
| **Karin magana / Waswasi** | Intrusive whisperings / Obsessive doubt | Obsessive-compulsive phenomena, paranoid ideation, auditory hallucinations. | Clarify if sounds are thoughts within the head or external voices. |
| **Tabin hankali / Hauka** | Disturbance of the mind / Madness | Psychosis, mania, delirium, or severe behavioral disturbance. | Immediate physical safety check; evaluate for cerebral malaria or meningitis. |
| **Ina ganin abubuwa / Ina jin muryoyi** | I am seeing things / I am hearing voices | Visual and auditory hallucinations (psychosis, drug withdrawal, severe malaria). | Perform full neurological exam and psychiatric evaluation. |
| **Kisan kai / Zan kashe kaina** | Suicide / I will kill myself | Active suicidal ideation; clinical emergency. | Immediate RED crisis protocol; do not leave patient alone. |

## 3. Nigerian Pidgin English (Broken) Clinical Idioms

| Pidgin Expression | Literal / Colloquial Meaning | Clinical Interpretation | Clinical Guidance |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Body dey pepper me / Fire dey burn inside me** | My body feels peppery / Internal burning sensation | Somatic depression, autonomic anxiety, visceral hyper-reactivity. | Validate complaint; check vital signs and malaria status; discuss mood. |
| **My head dey swell / Head wan scatter** | My head feels swollen / Head about to explode | Severe cognitive overload, acute stress reaction, tension headache, migraine. | Check blood pressure; assess acute crisis stressors and coping mechanisms. |
| **My heart dey do cut-and-join / My heart dey beat fast** | Arrhythmia feeling / Palpitations | Tachycardia from anxiety, panic attack, caffeine/kola nut excess. | Rule out arrhythmia; teach diaphragmatic square breathing. |
| **I no dey fit sleep / Night eye** | Insomnia / Wakefulness throughout the night | Terminal or middle insomnia; major depressive symptom or mania. | Assess sleep routine; check for intrusive thoughts or substance use. |
| **Everything just tire me / Life don tire me** | I am exhausted by everything / Life is wearisome | Passive suicidal ideation, hopelessness, severe depression. | Explicitly ask: *"You dey think say make you just sleep no wake up?"* |
| **I wan end am / Make I just die rest** | I want to end it / Let me just die to rest | Active suicidal intent; severe crisis level. | Trigger immediate Suicide Protocol; secure surroundings; alert family. |
| **Something dey push me** | An external force is urging me | Command hallucinations, severe obsessive impulse, or impulsive rage. | Red flag for patient or third-party safety; evaluate urgently. |
| **People dey talk for my back / Dem dey monitor me** | People are gossiping or tracking me | Persecutory delusions, paranoid psychosis, stimulant-induced paranoia. | Do not confront or affirm delusion directly; assess danger to self and others. |
| **Body weak like water** | Extreme profound fatigue | Severe depression, generalized exhaustion, electrolyte imbalance. | Full blood work, check PCV, investigate appetite and weight change. |
| **My eye dey turn me / Dizziness** | Vertigo or lightheadedness | Orthostatic hypotension, hyperventilation, anxiety attack, dehydration. | Check hydration, standing/sitting blood pressure, blood glucose. |

---

## 4. Practical CHEW Communication Strategies
1. **Never mock or invalidate somatic phrases**: Avoid saying "It's all in your head" or "Lab test is clean so nothing do you". Say: *"I understand say your body dey pain you truly. Stress and heavy heart fit make body burn like fire."*
2. **Translate to clear diagnostic criteria**: Use the glossary to map *"life don tire me"* directly into mhGAP Depressive Module assessment.
3. **Cross-cultural empathy**: Use bilingual framing to put the patient at ease before asking sensitive questions about self-harm or voices.

## 5. Arabic Language (العربية) Clinical Idioms (Northern Nigeria & Lake Chad Region)

| Arabic Expression | Literal Meaning | Clinical / Psychiatric Interpretation | Recommended Clinical Response |
|---|---|---|---|
| **نار في جسدي / حرارة داخلية** | Fire in my body / internal heat | Somatic depression, visceral hyper-reactivity (*zafin jiki* equivalent). | Check vital signs, rule out malaria/typhoid; validate emotional pain. |
| **تعبت من الحياة / أريد أن أرتاح** | I am tired of life / I want to rest | Passive to active suicidal ideation; severe depression. | Ask directly about self-harm plans; implement Suicide Protocol (Code Red/Yellow). |
| **أسمع أصواتاً / وسواس شديد** | I hear voices / severe whispering | Auditory hallucinations (psychosis, delirium) or intrusive OCD thoughts. | Assess for command hallucinations; rule out cerebral malaria and substance use. |
| **خفقان في القلب / ضيق شديد في الصدر** | Palpitations / severe chest tightness | Panic disorder, acute stress reaction, somatized anxiety. | Check pulse, BP, ECG/respiratory rate; initiate slow diaphragmatic breathing. |
| **صداع يفتت رأسي / رأسي يغلي** | Splitting headache / head boiling | Tension headache from severe cognitive/emotional stress. | Measure blood pressure; screen for sleep loss and chronic stressors. |
| **حمى شديدة مع تخليط في الكلام** | High fever with rambling/confusion | Organic delirium; acute medical emergency (cerebral malaria / meningitis). | Immediate Code Red emergency referral; urgent mRDT, blood sugar test. |

## 6. Igbo Language (Asụsụ Igbo) Clinical Idioms

| Igbo Expression | Literal Meaning | Clinical / Psychiatric Interpretation | Recommended Clinical Response |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Obi na-akụ m aka / Obi m na-ama jijiji** | My heart is beating me / heart shaking | Palpitations, panic attack, acute anxiety reaction. | Check pulse and BP; teach calm diaphragmatic breathing. |
| **Ike ọgwụgwụ / Ike adịghị m n'ahụ** | Total exhaustion / no strength in body | Severe anhedonia, vegetative depression, profound anemia. | Measure PCV/hemoglobin; assess for depressive episode. |
| **Isi na-awa m nke ukwuu / Ọkụ n'isi** | Severe head splitting / fire in head | Tension headache from chronic distress, somatized grief. | Check BP, sleep routine, and personal/financial stressors. |
| **Ihe na-akwanye m ime onwe m ihe ọjọọ** | Something is pushing me to do myself evil | Command hallucinations or severe self-harm impulse. | Emergency Code Red: remove dangerous items, continuous observation. |
| **Ndụ a agwụla m ike / Ka m nwụọ zuo ike** | Life has tired me / let me die and rest | Active or passive suicidal ideation; major depressive crisis. | Trigger suicide protocol; immediate supportive counseling. |
| **Ahụ ọkụ na-ekpo ọkụ na aghara aghara** | Very hot body with confusion | Organic delirium; severe malaria or meningitis rule-out. | Immediate mRDT test; urgent referral to General Hospital. |

## 7. Yoruba Language (Èdè Yorùbá) Clinical Idioms

| Yoruba Expression | Literal Meaning | Clinical / Psychiatric Interpretation | Recommended Clinical Response |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Inú mi ń bàjẹ́ / Ìrẹ̀wẹ̀sì ọkàn** | My stomach/inside is spoiled / soul downcast | Major depression, profound grief, depressive episode. | Assess mood, appetite, sleep; evaluate suicidal risk. |
| **Ọkàn mi ń lù kìkì / Ẹ̀rù ń bà mí** | Heart beating heavily / fear gripping me | Panic disorder, acute anxiety, visceral hyper-reactivity. | Check vitals, rule out arrhythmia/thyroid; slow breathing exercises. |
| **Orí mi ń fọ́ mi / Orí mi gbóná** | My head is splitting / my head is hot | Severe tension headache, somatization of acute distress. | Check blood pressure; assess sleep loss and emotional burdens. |
| **Nǹkan kan ń sọ fún mi láti pa ara mi** | Something is telling me to kill myself | Auditory command hallucinations; psychiatric emergency. | Immediate Code Red: safety precautions, do not leave patient alone. |
| **Ayé ti sú mi / Kí n kàn kú kí n sinmi** | I am tired of the world / let me just die | Expressed suicidal ideation; high suicide risk. | Immediate crisis de-escalation; involve family; referral. |
| **Ara ń gbóná pẹ̀lú ọ̀rọ̀ àbùkù / Ìyípadà èrò** | Hot body with incoherent speech / altered mind | Acute delirium; mandatory cerebral malaria / sepsis rule-out. | Urgent mRDT test, glucose check, immediate emergency transfer. |
